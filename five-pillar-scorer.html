The Market Gap Formula
Implied required growth = ( current P/E ÷ 20 ) ^ (1/5) − 1
Market gap = actual blended growth − implied required growth, capped between −5 and +5
A positive gap means the market is paying for less growth than the company delivers. A negative gap means the price requires more growth than is realistically achievable. The formula does not break at extreme P/E levels.
