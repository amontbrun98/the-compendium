On the correlation tagging

Each candidate is tagged with one of eight correlation profiles based on which macro and sentiment factors most directly drive its price. Tech Growth correlates with risk-on tech sentiment. Biotech correlates with binary clinical outcomes and FDA timing. Resources correlate with commodity cycles and inflation. Financial Disruption correlates with credit cycles. Consumer Brands correlate with consumer spending. Industrial Automation correlates with capex cycles. Space and Defense correlate with government spending. Emerging Markets correlate with currency and political risk. A diversified asymmetric sleeve picks from at least three different profiles.

Sources, calibration, and honest disclaimers

Confidence levels: Each company carries a confidence tag. HIGH means current data verified through Q4 2025 / Q1 2026 financials. MEDIUM means recent data with some category-based estimation. LOW means scoring relies meaningfully on category benchmarks rather than verified company-specific data. Treat LOW confidence scores as starting points for further research rather than verdicts.

Data: Revenue, growth, gross margins, and enterprise values from company filings, earnings calls, and analyst consensus through April 2026. International and small-cap names use approximations where direct data was limited.

Caveats: The framework rewards forward potential. Some candidates have negligible current revenue. The valuation pillar applies sector-appropriate benchmarks rather than universal metrics — a uranium miner is judged differently than a SaaS company.
