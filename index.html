№ 01 The Core Ledger Defensive

The Five Pillar Ledger

Quality, durability, growth, valuation, and regulatory exposure — measured for compounders.
Enterprises 31
Sectors 9
Pillars 5

Built to identify durable compounders trading at reasonable prices. Each enterprise is measured on the spread between return on invested capital and weighted average cost of capital, the durability of that spread, the growth trajectory, the price being paid, and the external regulatory exposure.

A vintage adjustment penalises companies whose quality is recently inflected — five years of excellence is not the same as twenty. A growth-implied market gap formula reveals where the market price departs most violently from the fundamentals.

№ 02 The Asymmetric Sleeve Opportunity

The Watch Opportunity

Market expansion, revenue trajectory, edge, capital efficiency, asymmetry, and valuation discipline — measured for becoming.
Candidates 36
Correlations 8
Pillars 6

The defensive ledger asks whether a business is durable today. The asymmetric ledger asks what it could become. Different question, different pillars. Each candidate is measured on market expansion potential, revenue trajectory, forming moats, capital efficiency, payoff asymmetry, and valuation reasonableness.

Tagged across eight correlation profiles — Tech Growth, Biotech, Resources, Financial, Consumer, Industrial, Space and Defense, and Emerging Markets — to surface genuinely uncorrelated bets rather than five flavours of the same exposure.
