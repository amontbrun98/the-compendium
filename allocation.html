№ 03 Auto-balance from Seed Tickers
What this does
Pick a handful of tickers you want exposure to. The engine sizes each one to respect every cap (per-name 8%/3%, per-cluster 25%/12%, Watch sleeve 20%, regime cash floor) and produces a balanced portfolio. It uses your sleeve assignment from the universe and weights names equally within their cap, then redistributes any clipped capital to under-weight names. Preview before committing.

№ 04 Stress Test Your Allocation
What this asks
"If X happens, what does my portfolio look like." Pick a scope (a single ticker, a cluster, a sleeve, or the whole portfolio) and apply a percentage shock. The result panel shows the new total, the dollar P&L, and the names that took the biggest hit. Use the preset buttons for canonical scenarios.
