A score does not size itself.

A 7+ on the Five-Pillar Ledger tells you a name is durable. It does not tell you how much to own. Without a sizing rule and a concentration rule, every concentrated thesis eventually becomes a single point of failure. This page imposes both — sleeve caps, name caps, cluster caps — and surfaces what the rules say about what you currently hold.

How it works
Edit data/positions.json with your actual positions. The engine reads the cluster tags from data/core.json and data/watch.json and tells you where you are concentrated against the rules. The rules are deliberately strict — better to fight them than to find out, after a 40% drawdown, that you weren't following any.
№ 01 The Sizing & Concentration Rules
RuleCap
Per-name cap, Core sleeve
No single durable compounder gets more than 8% of capital. Even Lilly has had 40% drawdowns on regulatory news.

Per-name cap, Watch sleeve
Asymmetric bets are sized to survive a zero. 3% per name means you can be wrong on five in a row and still have most of your capital.
 3%
Per-cluster cap, Core sleeve
Cluster = correlated thesis (e.g. mag7_platforms, glp1_franchise, payments_network). Six durable compounders in the same cluster is one bet, not six.
 25%
Per-cluster cap, Watch sleeve
Same logic, tighter bound. Two gene-editing names is one biotech bet.
 12%
Total Watch sleeve cap
The asymmetric sleeve is high-variance by design. Capping it at 20% of total capital means a 100% loss across the whole sleeve is still survivable.
 20%
Cash floor
Dry powder is its own position. Adjusts up in stagflation/deflation regimes (Q3/Q4), down in goldilocks (Q1).
 10–25%
