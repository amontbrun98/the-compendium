Read this first — what this page can and can't show

This is not a true backtest of the framework. The composite score combines live volatile inputs (P/E, PEG, FCF Yield) with curated inputs (ROIC, WACC, moat, vintage, regulatory). The curated inputs are frozen at the most recent quarterly review and cannot be recomputed historically — we don't have a time-series of them. So we can't replay the framework's score at every point in the past.

What we can show: for the universe of names ranked today, did the ones the framework currently rates highly actually compound over the prior 1, 3, and 5 years? Did "Strong" verdicts outperform "Watch" or "Speculative"? That's what the table and chart below answer.

Survivorship bias. The universe is "names still in the ledger today." Companies that scored well five years ago and went bankrupt or got delisted aren't in the dataset. The historical returns of survivors are systematically higher than what an investor would have realised running the framework live. Treat this as a sanity check, not as evidence.

Curated-input freeze. ROIC at 49% for Apple is a 2026 reading. Five years ago it was different. Applying current ROIC to a 5-year backtest is anachronistic — it asks "did names that look good now have done well historically," not "did names that looked good then do well."

What it's still useful for. If "Strong" verdicts have systematically lower 5-year returns than "Watch" verdicts, the framework's calibration is broken — that would show up here. If "Strong" outperforms by a wide margin, that's encouraging but not conclusive. The honest reading is somewhere in between.

What's in this page

For each company in both ledgers, the framework's current composite score, current verdict, and the total return over standard windows ending on the asof date. SPY is included as the equity baseline. Aggregates by verdict band show whether high-scoring names are clustered in the upper-right of the scatter (good) or scattered all over (calibration weak).

Sources & methodology

Returns: total return computed from yfinance auto-adjusted closes (dividends reinvested). 3y and 5y returns are annualised; shorter windows are total. Composite scores: live ledger data, embedded snapshot values for curated fields, daily-refreshed price multiples from prices.json. Disclaimer: Educational framework only. Past performance is not predictive of future results. Verify everything before acting.
